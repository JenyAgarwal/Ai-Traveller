Mathematical Precision

Clear Breakdown of Every Rupee

No hidden fees, no sudden surprise bills. Every trip forecast generated on AI Traveller follows a strict, audited mathematical formula accounting for the realities of group travel.

1. Distance-Scaled Transport

Calculates round-trip costs per traveller based on distance between origin and destination and your selected transit mode.

2. Capacity-Based Rooms

3. Daily Dining & Local Transit

Allocates realistic per-person meal estimates and group day cab/auto fares for the full trip duration.

4. Sightseeing & Attraction Fees

5. 10% Emergency Reserve

Automatically reserves 10% of total planned expenses to protect against spontaneous costs or surge rates.
